# Story Builder

**Skill:** Verbal Memory
**Level:** 1

## Rules

Read a four sentence everyday story at your own pace. Recall the same story with three missing words. For each blank, choose one of three large options. Correct and incorrect choices receive immediate feedback before you continue. There is no timer and no penalty for an incorrect answer.

## Level settings

- One story per play, randomly selected from 15 everyday stories.
- Four short sentences, three blanks, and three answer options per blank.
- Untimed.

## Scoring

Score is the percentage of correctly recalled words, rounded to the nearest whole number: 3 correct = 100, 2 = 67, 1 = 33, 0 = 0.
